The wild around us

A snipe hiding in the reeds. A duck preening on a city pond. A parakeet hanging upside down from a tree in the park. An ibex just above a mountain town. These animals live right next to us, every day, but most of the time we walk past without seeing them.

That's why I wanted to give you a piece of my work: animals we can find in cities, parks and mountains, that can become part of your home.

Photography for everyone

Professional, artistic photography often feels out of reach, kept for galleries, books and collectors. I want it to be available to everyone. Every photograph is printed to order in Barcelona, on high-quality paper, at a fair price, so a piece of nature can hang on any wall.
