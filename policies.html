Shipping & returns

Shipping

Every print is made to order in Barcelona and ships within 5–7 working days, carefully packed in a rigid tube. We ship worldwide, and shipping is free on orders over €75.

Returns

You can return your print within 14 days of delivery, in its original condition. Email us first and we'll send you the instructions.

Damaged prints

If your print arrives damaged, send us a photo within 7 days and we'll send a replacement free of charge.

Privacy

We only use your details to process and ship your order. Card payments are handled securely by Stripe; we never see your card details.
