Our story

The wild lives next door.

We live busy city lives. Metro, work, traffic, screens. And yet, just a few metres away, there's a whole other world going on.

01

Stop and look

A snipe hiding in the reeds a few minutes from Barcelona. A duck preening on a city pond. A spider building its web on a balcony plant. They live with us every day, but most of the time we walk past without seeing them.

Paolo Laitinen started photographing them almost by accident, and once you start looking, you can't stop seeing them. That's what these photographs are about: what you see when you stop for a moment and look around.

02

BarcelonaWhere we live, and where the shop is based.

ChamonixWhere the mountains and the forest meet the town.
03

Always on the road

We love to travel. Our journey through Latin America took us through rainforests, mountains and coasts, with the camera always ready. Those photographs will become our next collection, coming soon.

04

Fine art for everyone

Professional nature photography usually lives in galleries, books and expensive frames. We think it should be on everyone's walls. That's why every photograph is printed on museum-quality paper, made to order in Barcelona, at a price that makes sense.
